# 30-day learning plan — helping Pymes adopt AI

**Goal by day 30:** you can run a paid AI diagnostic end to end, and you have 3 founding clients booked.

**Time budget:** about 60–90 minutes a day on weekdays, around your contract work, plus one longer block on Saturday. Sundays off.

**The rule:** learn by doing. Every week ends with something real: an interview, a demo, a workshop, a client conversation. Reading is support, not the goal.

Use with [[ai-diagnostic-template]], which is the method you'll practice all month.

---

## Week 1 — Understand how a Pyme actually runs

Without this, everything else is guessing. The diagnostic is mostly listening.

- [ ] **Day 1 —** Read [[ai-diagnostic-template]] end to end. Write down the 3 questions you feel least sure how to ask.
- [ ] **Day 2 —** Read *The Mom Test* (Rob Fitzpatrick), first half. It teaches how to ask about past behavior instead of opinions ("walk me through the last time you…").
- [ ] **Day 3 —** Finish *The Mom Test*. Rewrite 5 questions from the template in that style.
- [ ] **Day 4 —** Learn the basic map of a business: sales → quotes → orders → invoicing → collections → customer service → reporting. For each step, list 2 tasks that eat hours in a typical 20–100 person company.
- [ ] **Day 5 —** Pick 2 practice businesses you know (family, church, friends, an ex-colleague's company). Ask for 45 minutes each; tell them it's free and you're practicing.
- [ ] **Saturday —** Run practice interview #1 with the template. Fill the process inventory table, even if it's rough.

**Done when:** you have one filled process inventory from a real business.

---

## Week 2 — Know the tools your clients already have

Often the best answer is "turn on what you already pay for." You need to know these tools as an admin, not just as a user.

- [ ] **Day 8 —** ChatGPT for business: workspaces, projects, custom GPTs, sharing, and the data and privacy settings an admin controls. Write a one-page cheat sheet.
- [ ] **Day 9 —** Claude for teams: projects, shared knowledge, connectors, admin and data settings. Same cheat sheet format.
- [ ] **Day 10 —** Microsoft 365 Copilot and Gemini in Google Workspace: what each does inside email, docs and spreadsheets, and what a Pyme needs to pay for and turn on. Note which suite each practice business uses.
- [ ] **Day 11 —** Build a "use case library": 3 concrete examples each for sales, admin/finance, customer service, HR and operations. Each one says the task, the tool, the prompt or setup, and the time it saves.
- [ ] **Day 12 —** Run practice interview #2. Use the use case library to suggest ideas live, and note which ones made the owner lean in.
- [ ] **Saturday —** Write the full diagnostic report for practice business #1 using the deliverable structure in the template. Time yourself.

**Done when:** you have a written report you'd be comfortable charging for.

---

## Week 3 — Automation and data privacy

Chat tools save minutes; automations save hours. Privacy answers build trust fast.

- [ ] **Day 15 —** Pick one automation tool and stay with it: n8n (more control, self-hostable) or Make (faster to learn). Do the official beginner tutorial.
- [ ] **Day 16 —** Build demo automation #1: a web form or email lead → spreadsheet or CRM → an AI-drafted reply for a human to approve.
- [ ] **Day 17 —** Learn the WhatsApp Business Platform basics: what needs Meta approval, message templates, and costs. In Mexico this is where customers and sales live.
- [ ] **Day 18 —** Build demo automation #2: summarize incoming emails or WhatsApp messages into a daily digest for the owner.
- [ ] **Day 19 —** Data privacy: read a plain-language summary of Mexico's personal data law (the Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares; check the current version in the DOF). Write your own one-page "what goes into AI tools and what doesn't" rules for clients.
- [ ] **Saturday —** Build demo automation #3: a weekly report built from a spreadsheet, written up by AI. Record a 2-minute screen video of each demo for sales calls.

**Done when:** you have 3 working demos and a privacy one-pager.

---

## Week 4 — Teach, measure, and sell

- [ ] **Day 22 —** Design a 3-hour workshop: 20 minutes on what AI is and isn't, 2 hours hands-on with the team's own tasks, 40 minutes building their playbook. Adults learn by doing, so keep slides to under 10.
- [ ] **Day 23 —** Build an ROI calculator in a spreadsheet: hours per week × people × hourly cost, before vs. after. Use it in every report.
- [ ] **Day 24 —** Write your outreach message (WhatsApp and email) for the founding-client offer: a diagnostic at about 50% off in exchange for a testimonial and permission to publish results.
- [ ] **Day 25 —** Make a list of 10 Pymes (20–300 people) you can reach through a warm introduction. Send 5 messages.
- [ ] **Day 26 —** Send the other 5. Practice your discovery call out loud: ask about pain first, propose nothing in the first 15 minutes.
- [ ] **Saturday —** Run the workshop for practice business #1 or #2, even if it's free. Ask them what was most useful and what confused them.

**Done when:** you've run one workshop and sent 10 outreach messages.

---

## Days 29–30 — Review

- [ ] Count: practice interviews done, reports written, demos built, messages sent, diagnostics booked.
- [ ] Update [[ai-diagnostic-template]] with what you learned from using it.
- [ ] Write down the 3 things a client asked you that you couldn't answer. Those are next month's learning plan.

**Target:** 3 founding diagnostics booked. If you have fewer, send 10 more messages before learning anything new.

---

## Keep doing after day 30

- Every diagnostic gets measured: a baseline before you change anything, the same number after.
- Every result becomes a case study on jesusrtech.com, with the client's OK.
- Raise your prices after each new published case study.
