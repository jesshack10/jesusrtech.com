Welcome to my corner of the internet.

This site is my professional home — a place to share the projects I'm working on, document things I learn, and give potential collaborators and employers a clear picture of who I am and what I build.

## What to expect

- **Projects** — detailed write-ups on things I've shipped
- **Blog** — technical posts, opinions, and lessons learned
- **CV** — my professional background, always up to date
- **Uses** — the tools and gear I rely on every day

## The stack

I built this with [Astro](https://astro.build) and Tailwind CSS. The whole site is statically generated, which means it's fast, secure, and cheap to host. Blog posts and project pages are just Markdown files — no CMS needed.
