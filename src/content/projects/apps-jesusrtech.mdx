## Why I built it

Good gatherings stall on logistics: someone has to deal cards, keep secrets, count votes, or remember who said what. I wanted to see how much of that friction software could remove — so the people in the room spend their time talking to each other instead of managing the game.

The rule for every app: **anyone at the table can join in seconds, from the phone already in their pocket.**

## The apps

- **Impostor** — every player secretly gets the same word, except one: the impostor. Players describe the word without giving it away and try to spot who's bluffing. The app deals the words privately to each phone from a bank of more than 300.
- **Werewolf** — the classic social-deduction game with werewolves, a seer, a doctor, and villagers. The app helps balance roles for the group size, deals them in secret, guides the moderator through each night, and runs the votes.
- **Peticiones** — a request exchange for groups and events. Everyone submits a personal request, anonymously if they prefer, and the app assigns each participant someone else's request to support — no papers, no hat, no one seeing who wrote what.
- **Pomodoro** — a focus timer for study and work sessions, with an intention for each session and calm visual feedback (an hourglass, a growing plant, a painting that fills in) instead of a ticking clock.

## How it works

- **Join by QR code or 6-digit code.** The host's screen shows a QR code and a short code; players scan or type it and are in. Players are signed in anonymously behind the scenes, so nobody creates an account.
- **Real-time sync.** Game state lives in Firebase Realtime Database, so every phone updates instantly when a role is dealt, a vote is cast, or a round ends. Each phone shows only its own secret word or role.
- **A shared rooms module.** Lobbies, join flows, player lists, kicking, and generated animal avatars live in one reusable module, so a new game starts from a working multiplayer foundation instead of from scratch.
- **Sessions clean up after themselves.** Sessions expire after an hour, and session codes are claimed atomically so two groups never collide.
- **Installable.** It's a progressive web app — open it once and add it to the home screen.

## What it says about how I work

The same instinct drives my professional work: find the friction, then remove it with software that gets out of the way. Here the "users" are friends, families, and event groups — and the measure of success is that nobody notices the technology at all.
