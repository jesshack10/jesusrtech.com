## Overview

An IoT platform that combined humidity, temperature, and heart-rate sensors to measure biological signals in real time.

## Recognition

The prototype won the **Intel challenge** at the *Simposium Internacional de Sistemas Computacionales y Tecnologías de Información (SISCTI) 40*.
